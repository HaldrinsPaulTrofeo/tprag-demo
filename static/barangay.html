How the office decides how much each barangay receives

Vaccine supply is limited, so the office does not split it by guesswork. For each anti-rabies request, the system recommends the smallest of three numbers:

  1. What past sessions needed: the number of vials that was enough for 3 out of 4 recorded sessions (your barangay's own sessions once it has at least six, otherwise all of Pagsanjan's).
  2. What is left in supply after barangays ahead in the queue are served in full.
  3. What you requested.

If supply cannot cover a full session, the request is deferred to the next delivery instead of being given a token amount. The Municipal Agriculture Office makes the final decision and records its reason, which you can see above.

Livestock, seeds, fingerlings and other inputs are recorded and reviewed by the office directly; there is no past data yet to base a recommendation on.
